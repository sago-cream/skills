# Attribution and extraction

The decision board was extracted from this repository's `design-grind` skill, whose review cycle adapts Theo Browne's [council](https://github.com/t3dotgg/tstack/blob/1085050f3baed1ac724caa4a8166145fb2ac88af/skills/council/SKILL.md). The [complete MIT notice](LICENSE) is retained with this skill.

The board draws on this repository's `userflow-review` catalog/packaging approach and the ccxpLite adoption review process. This skill owns the viewer, builder, catalog reference, and tests, and adds a standalone capture-and-decision workflow. `design-grind` invokes it after scored design reviews.
