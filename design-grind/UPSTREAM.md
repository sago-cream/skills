# Attribution and adaptation

The review cycle adapts Theo Browne's [council](https://github.com/t3dotgg/tstack/blob/1085050f3baed1ac724caa4a8166145fb2ac88af/skills/council/SKILL.md), pinned to tstack commit `1085050f3baed1ac724caa4a8166145fb2ac88af`. The [complete MIT notice](LICENSE) travels with this skill.

The adaptation retains read-only scored feedback and fresh reviews after fixes. It adds locally saved reviewer settings, UI state coverage, the design rubric, a passing target, and a deterministic before/after board with saved accept/decline choices and independent notes. The board uses the stable catalog and offline packaging approach from this repository's `userflow-review`, with change-level decisions drawn from the ccxpLite adoption review process.
